## Short announcement

I’ve released Market Opportunity Research, an open-source Agent Skill for investigating business ideas.

It guides an AI agent from buyer problems and demand evidence through real competitors and commercial tradeoffs to a small experiment that tests willingness to pay. The repository includes a fictional sample so you can see the output before installing.

It’s MIT-licensed and the skill package contains no executable scripts. Current research still needs browsing, source checking, and real customer validation.

https://github.com/itstinatao/market-opportunity-research

I’d welcome feedback on the evidence standards and whether the proposed experiments are useful in practice.
